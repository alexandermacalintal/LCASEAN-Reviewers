# LCASEAN — Practice examination (working edition)

**Scope:** Modules 01–04 plus working module 05's Goh, Almonte and Acharya readings. This is substantial practice for the verified material, **not yet a comprehensive examination of all supplied resources**. Economic/common-currency, full socio-cultural/identity/media and maritime/web coverage remains pending. Suggested time: 60–75 minutes. Answers and reasoning are in ANSWER_KEY.md.

## Part I — Multiple choice (1 point each)

1. Which statement best describes ASEAN's regional foundations?
   A. A single language and religion made cooperation inevitable.
   B. Diverse states constructed institutions to address shared concerns.
   C. ASEAN replaced all precolonial political identities.
   D. Regional boundaries were uncontested before independence.

2. ASEAN was established by:
   A. The Bangkok Declaration, 8 August 1967.
   B. The TAC, February 1967.
   C. Bali Concord II, 1976.
   D. The Charter's entry into force, 2007.

3. What is the most accurate description of the founding purposes?
   A. Collective military defense alone.
   B. Tariff reduction alone.
   C. Connected developmental, peaceful, social, cultural and technical aims.
   D. Immediate replacement of national governments.

4. Which pairing is correct?
   A. ASEAN Chair — nonrenewable five-year term.
   B. Secretary-General — annual alphabetical chairmanship.
   C. Secretary-General — nonrenewable five-year term.
   D. Community Council — supreme policymaking body above the Summit.

5. Charter Article 3's legal personality means:
   A. ASEAN is automatically a supranational government.
   B. ASEAN exists as an intergovernmental organization with legal personality.
   C. Members no longer need domestic implementation.
   D. The Secretary-General can unilaterally override national law.

6. The ACC mainly:
   A. Issues binding judgments on individual rights complaints.
   B. Coordinates across pillars and prepares summits.
   C. Replaces national education ministries.
   D. Commands a regional standing army.

7. ASEAN Minus X is best understood as:
   A. Any minority may impose any regional policy.
   B. Economic implementation flexibility where members agree to use it.
   C. Automatic expulsion of dissenting members.
   D. Abolition of consensus in every policy area.

8. Which claim about dispute settlement is most accurate?
   A. ASEAN has no dispute-settlement provisions.
   B. Every disagreement automatically receives a judicial penalty.
   C. The Charter contains procedures, but political referral is not automatic punishment.
   D. Every dispute must follow the 2003 annex's stages in mandatory order.

9. AICHR should be described as:
   A. Necessarily a court with binding individual judgments.
   B. A human-rights institution whose mandate must be distinguished from a court's.
   C. The regional finance authority.
   D. The military command of the APSC.

10. Which statement follows from Concord II?
   A. A security community is necessarily a defense pact.
   B. Political-security, economic and socio-cultural cooperation reinforce each other.
   C. Socio-cultural cooperation is irrelevant to security.
   D. A common currency had already been established.

11. Goh's main conceptual distinction is between:
   A. ASEAN's diplomatic procedures and nonintervention as a principle.
   B. The Summit and the World Bank.
   C. A single currency and a customs union.
   D. Malaysia and Malaya as identical historical entities.

12. Why is Cambodia's membership status relevant to Goh's case?
   A. Cambodia founded ASEAN.
   B. Cambodia was not an ASEAN member at the conflict's outbreak.
   C. Vietnam was chairing ASEAN during the invasion.
   D. China was bound by all later Charter membership provisions in 1978.

13. Which statement accurately treats Almonte's text?
   A. A peacekeeping proposal proves a regional force was created by 2012.
   B. The 2015 reprint converts its 2004 forecasts into verified results.
   C. Proposed institutions and target dates remain proposals unless outcomes are separately verified.
   D. Almonte says political trust is irrelevant to cooperation.

14. Acharya explains centrality partly through:
   A. ASEAN's military superiority over all external powers.
   B. External powers finding an ASEAN-led platform mutually acceptable.
   C. ASEAN's elimination of every national foreign policy.
   D. A legal ban on non-ASEAN regional institutions.

15. Which is strongest evidence of centrality of substance?
   A. ASEAN's name appears in a meeting title.
   B. A summit photograph places the chair in the middle.
   C. ASEAN helps set an agenda and resolve a documented regional disagreement.
   D. A declaration merely repeats the word centrality.

## Part II — Correct the claim (3 points each)

For each, identify the error and supply a corrected statement with an explanation.

16. “The TAC was adopted in February 1967, as the Structures slide states.”
17. “The Charter was signed and entered into force on the same day in 2007.”
18. “The ASEAN Way means ASEAN never applies diplomatic pressure.”
19. “Because all major powers attend ASEAN-led meetings, ASEAN controls their foreign policies.”
20. “A Vision 2025 target is evidence that the target was met.”

## Part III — Short answers (5 points each)

21. Explain how colonial experience and postcolonial state-building help account for ASEAN's attachment to sovereignty.
22. Distinguish the Summit, ACC, Community Councils and Secretariat by function.
23. Explain one benefit and one limitation of consensus, and qualify your answer using Minus X.
24. Explain why Goh's Cambodia–Haiti comparison cannot prove that one conflict-management model always works better.
25. Explain Almonte's link between political will, trust and practical cooperation.
26. State Acharya's four challenges to centrality in the 2017 article. Distinguish their historical setting from claims about the present.

## Part IV — Applied questions (10 points each)

27. **A regional emergency:** A domestic conflict displaces people into neighboring states. Some governments favor quiet relief coordination; others want public condemnation and binding intervention. Explain the sovereignty/cooperation tension, identify relevant institutional levels without inventing an automatic command power, and distinguish diplomatic activity from successful protection.

28. **A centrality claim:** A student writes, “ASEAN hosted a forum, therefore it resolved major-power rivalry.” Evaluate this inference using Acharya. Specify the additional evidence needed and explain why external acceptance and internal cohesion matter.

## Part V — Integrative essay (20 points)

29. “ASEAN's respect for sovereignty is both the foundation of cooperation and a constraint on deeper regional action.” Discuss using at least one country-history connection, the Charter's institutions/decision rules, and one of the three readings in module 05. Distinguish formal commitments, author arguments and demonstrated outcomes. Include one counterargument.

**Suggested scoring:** Part I 15; II 15; III 30; IV 20; V 20 = 100 points. Reward explanation and accurate qualification, not just acronym lists. The question set is thematic practice, not a forecast of the instructor's examination.
